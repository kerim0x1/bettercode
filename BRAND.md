## The name

Write the product name as **BetterC0de**, with a capital B, a capital C, and the digit zero in C0de. The website is [betterc0de.com](https://betterc0de.com).

Use the full name on first mention. Keep the same spelling in headings, download labels, social posts, and support messages.

## The message

**Primary tagline**

> Your coding agents. One desktop workspace.

**One-sentence description**

> BetterC0de is a desktop AI coding environment that brings your agents, files, terminals, and live previews into one workspace.

**Short product introduction**

> Build with your preferred AI coding tools in a workspace designed around the project. BetterC0de brings conversations, editing, Git review, and browser previews together, with Editor, Agent, and Canvas modes for different ways of working.

## The mark

The [included logo](assets/betterc0de-mark.png) is the existing BetterC0de app icon: four offset horizontal bars in a rounded, dark tile. Use this asset consistently across these documents and product introductions.

Preserve its square proportions, original colors, and internal spacing. Leave clear space around the mark and keep text outside the tile. Use the product name alongside the icon when introducing BetterC0de to a new audience.

## Visual direction

Keep the presentation calm, precise, and focused on the work. Use a dark base, warm white, and restrained grays inspired by the existing icon.

| Color | Value | Suggested use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Near black | #0A0A0A | Dark surfaces and brand backgrounds. |
| Warm white | #F7F5F2 | Primary text and prominent marks on dark surfaces. |
| Light gray | #B5B5B5 | Secondary visual elements. |
| Mid gray | #666666 | Supporting graphic details. |

These are presentation guidelines for this documentation set, rather than a complete application theme specification. Keep text contrast readable on each background.

### In-product UI

The product interface follows the [UI and theme guide](docs/design-system.md). Use its semantic color tokens and shared components rather than placing the brand palette above the user's light, dark, system, or imported theme. For application icons and installer art, start from the existing [favicon](apps/ui/public/favicon.svg), [desktop icons](apps/shell/build/icon.png), and [mobile icon generation](apps/mobile/README.md); do not recolor or redraw the mark independently for a feature.

For Markdown, rely on the reader's native typography. Use short paragraphs, descriptive headings, generous spacing, and tables where they make comparisons clearer. Let the logo and writing carry the identity.

## Voice

Be direct, helpful, and specific. Explain what someone can do and how to get started. Prefer concrete words such as **open**, **choose**, **build**, **preview**, and **review**.

Describe beta features and platform availability accurately. Avoid promises of perfect AI output, universal provider support, unlimited usage, or completely offline operation.
