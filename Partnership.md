# Sponsorship & Partnerships

BetterC0de welcomes sponsors who want to support the project's continued development and maintenance.

I have personally invested **more than $25,000** in this project. Sponsorship proposals should reflect the work and resources behind it.

## Sponsorship contributions

You can sponsor this project with a contribution of:

- **$500 USD**
- **$2,500 USD**
- **$5,000 USD**

Sponsorship duration and partnership terms are agreed individually.

## Before you reach out

I use **three Codex 20× accounts and two Claude 20× accounts** to keep this project running. Maintaining BetterC0de requires a substantial ongoing investment.

**Please do not contact me with unsolicited $20, $30, $50, or $300 API-credit offers.** These offers do not meet the project's sponsorship requirements.

Sponsorship inquiries should start at **$500 USD** and include a concrete proposal. Please respect my time by avoiding generic promotional pitches and repeated low-value offers.

## Contact

For sponsorship and partnership inquiries, email **[contact@kerim0x1.com](mailto:contact@kerim0x1.com)**.

Include your name or company, your proposed contribution, the intended duration, and any expectations you have for the partnership.
